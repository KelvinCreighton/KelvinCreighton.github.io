About Me

I'm Kelvin Creighton. I studied Computer Science at the University of Alberta, where I developed an interest in embedded systems, firmware, and low-level software. I enjoy working close to the hardware and understanding what happens beneath the abstractions.

How I Work

Curiosity drives the process.

I learn by building, experimenting, and figuring things out along the way. I prefer understanding why something works over simply knowing how to use it. That curiosity has taken me from programming microcontrollers to exploring operating systems, robotics, and security.
