How to Play

Core Mechanics

Unlike traditional platformers, you place "Powers" at your cursor to change your velocity.

  • Mouse - Position your power placement
  • Keys Q, W, E, D, C, X, Z, A - Place directional powers
  • S Key - Placement clear power

Other Controls

  • Spacebar - Instantly trigger a control swap (Primarily for testing)
